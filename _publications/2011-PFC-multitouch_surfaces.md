The project built VideaLAB's infrared, computer-vision multi-touch table from the hardware up: prototype, control software and interaction paradigms for 3D exploration, later used for the [interactive inspection of the Cathedral of Santiago](/publications/2012-VAR-inspeccion_catedral.html) and in a permanent exhibition at the University of A Coruña.
