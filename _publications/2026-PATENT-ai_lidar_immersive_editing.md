US patent application filed in November 2025 and published in June 2026 with the Hamad Bin Khalifa University team. The claims describe a pipeline that starts from a single spherical image of an indoor scene and chains clutter removal, multitask dense prediction, indoor style transfer with a hybrid attention mechanism, an editing module and automatic stereo generation, so that a captured room can be emptied, restyled, edited collaboratively and viewed immersively. It builds on our work on [indoor modeling from panoramic images](/publications/2024-PhD-pintore_immersive_indoor_omnidirectional.html). The application is pending; the full text is on [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20260154782A1/en).
