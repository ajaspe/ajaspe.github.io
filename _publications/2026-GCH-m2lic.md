abstract: "Multi-Light Image Collections (MLIC) are fundamental for virtual re-lighting and surface analysis in cultural heritage. However, most standard free-form acquisition remains hindered by the need for calibration targets, specialized training, and manual processing. We present M²LIC (Mobile Multi-Light Image Collection), a fully automated, target-free pipeline utilizing just two commodity smartphones, i.e., one fixed for capture, one moving for illumination. Our framework synchronizes video streams, performs frame selection to identify a small set of sharp and well-distributed images, infers light directions directly from the scene structure, and automatically generates web-ready relightable models. Validated on diverse artifacts, this rapid, open-source protocol significantly lowers the barrier to entry, enabling highly scalable MLIC digitization workflows."
