This is the blog part of the Desk. It will be a space for small opinions and notes that do not fit anywhere else on the site: things about research, about building visualizations, about tools, sometimes about nothing in particular.

I would like it to have the wonderful smell of the 2000's internet: a personal page, written by hand, with no algorithm deciding who reads it and no metrics to chase. Posts will come when there is something to say, which may be every few weeks or every few months.

There are no comments here. When a post has a "Discuss on LinkedIn" button, that is the place where I will read what you think. And there is an [Atom feed](/feed.xml), like in the old days :)
