A Gaussian splat (a 3D model that looks like a photo, made from many images of a scene) is only as good as the video you give it. The software has to see every part of the scene from several angles, sharp, and with nothing changing between frames. A normal phone is enough for that, if you film with it in mind.

First the rules that always apply, then what changes depending on what you film. They come from our own captures, around 30 of them, from museum objects to ruins under the desert sun, plus the usual advice for photogrammetry.

The basics for any capture

Moving

{% include splat-guide/card.html icon="move" badge="ok" title="Walk all around" text="the 3D comes from your steps; turning on the spot gives nothing" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="slow" title="Slow and steady" text="a calm walking pace; fast moves blur the frames" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="frame" badge="ok" title="Keep it in frame" text="the whole time; no sudden turns, no looking away" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="loop" badge="ok" title="End where you started" text="a full circle if you can; closing the loop joins everything" %}

The scene

{% include splat-guide/card.html icon="light" badge="ok" title="Soft, even light" text="daylight or diffuse light; no strong shadows, no backlight" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="still" badge="bad" title="Nothing moving" text="people or cars crossing, pets, leaves in the wind" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="glass" badge="bad" title="Avoid glass and shine" text="reflections change with every angle; if you can't avoid them, pick angles with less glare" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="texture" title="Texture around it" text="a plain white table or a blank wall leaves nothing to match between frames" %}

The phone

{% include splat-guide/card.html icon="4k" badge="ok" title="4K, 30 or 60 fps" text="30 is enough; 60 gives sharper frames, but only with good light" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="zoom" badge="bad" title="Don't zoom" text="move closer with your feet, and keep the same lens all the video" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="lock" badge="ok" title="Lock focus and exposure" text="long-press on the screen, so the brightness does not jump" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="modes" badge="ok" title="Plain video mode" text="normal stabilisation is fine; no Action, Cinematic or Portrait mode: they warp, crop or blur the image" %}

An object on a table

A vase, a shoe, a toy, a small sculpture...

  • 20–60 s
  • 2 or 3 rounds
  • fills half the frame

Walk slowly around it at its height (1), then a second round higher, looking down at it (2). That gets the top and the sides. A third, lower one (3) if you can. Same distance all the way.

{% include splat-guide/card.html icon="walkaround" title="Room to walk around" text="the middle of a table or a stool, not against a wall" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="base" badge="ok" title="A textured base" text="a patterned cloth, a newspaper or wood under it" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="half" badge="ok" title="Fill half the frame" text="close enough for detail, with some background to track" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="shadow" badge="bad" title="Watch your shadow" text="with the light behind you, your shadow walks over it" %}
{% include splat-guide/plan-outdoors.svg %}

A big thing outdoors

A statue, a car, a big rock, a fountain, the front of a building...

  • 40–90 s
  • 2 rounds, far and close
  • cloudy, or all in shade

First a round far enough to see all of it (1), then a closer one for the details (2). If it is tall, lift the phone over your head for part of the way.

{% include splat-guide/card.html icon="lesssky" badge="ok" title="Less sky" text="point a bit down; the sky has no depth and confuses the model" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="armsup" badge="ok" title="Arms up for the top" text="part of the round with the phone over your head" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="arc" title="Can't go around it?" text="a facade or a cliff: walk arcs in front of it, near and far" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="wind" badge="bad" title="Mind the wind" text="leaves and grass that move come out blurry" %}
{% include splat-guide/plan-room.svg %}

A room

A living room, an office, a lab, a small museum hall...

  • 1–2 min
  • along the walls, then closer
  • every light on

Walk along the walls looking across the room, slowly at the corners. Then one more pass, closer, for the furniture and anything the first one missed.

{% include splat-guide/card.html icon="across" badge="ok" title="Look across the room" text="from near the walls; never spin on the spot in the middle" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="lights" badge="ok" title="Every light on" text="before you start, and don't touch them while filming" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="screens" badge="bad" title="Mirrors and screens" text="they look like windows to another room; cover them" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="ceiling" badge="ok" title="Ceiling and floor too" text="tilt up and down a bit as you walk, or they will be holes" %}
{% include splat-guide/plan-place.svg %}

A whole place

A street, a courtyard, a garden, some ruins, a building from outside...

  • 2–3 min
  • there and back, a loop
  • steady light, not at sunset

Walk the street (or the path) looking at one side, and come back looking at the other side, so you finish where you started. The phone a bit turned, not straight ahead.

{% include splat-guide/card.html icon="sides" badge="ok" title="Look to the sides" text="what is straight ahead barely moves between frames, so it gives little 3D" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="corner" badge="ok" title="Slow at corners and doors" text="turn while you keep walking; this is where the track gets lost" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="norec" title="Don't stop recording" text="the walk between two spots is what joins them" %} {% include splat-guide/card.html icon="auto" badge="ok" title="Exposure on auto here" text="from sun to shade, a locked exposure burns or darkens half the video" %}

Before and after

Before you press record

  • Clean the lens. A fingerprint puts a haze over the whole model.
  • Battery and free space, 4K fills the phone fast.
  • 4K at 30 or 60 fps, and HDR video off (in many tools its frames come out washed out).
  • Decide the route: where you start and where you finish.

Before you leave

  • Watch the whole video on the phone. Blurry parts? Somebody crossing? A side you forgot?
  • If in doubt, film it again. One more minute now is much cheaper than coming back.
  • Send the original file. WhatsApp and similar apps compress the video a lot, unless you send it as a document.
